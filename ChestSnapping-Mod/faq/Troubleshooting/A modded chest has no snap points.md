A modded chest has no snap points

Add its prefab name to ExtraPrefabs in the config, or turn on AutoDetectContainers (off by default) so every buildable container gets snap points. Use ExcludedPrefabs for anything you do not want. The chestsnap console commands (chestsnap_list, chestsnap_add) exist only while the developer's KydlTools mod is loaded.
